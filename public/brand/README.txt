RAFFAELLA GALLO — LOGO FILES
Treatment 3b (Playfair Display, spaced caps)

VECTOR (svg/)
logo-horizontal        primary. website header, email signature, letterhead
logo-badge             circle lockup. social profile, stamps, stickers
logo-badge-reversed    white on anthracite
logo-badge-black       one-colour black. embroidery, stamps, fax-grade print
logo-stacked           silhouette over name, no circle. flyers, posters
logo-mark              circle + silhouette, no text
logo-mark-black        one-colour
logo-mark-white        for dark backgrounds (transparent)
logo-monogram          RG in circle. embroidery, dog tags, small applications
logo-monogram-black    one-colour
favicon                RG, solid anthracite disc
silhouette             the mark alone, ink / white / black

RASTER (png/) — 2000 px, transparent where the design allows

COLOUR
anthracite   #25282A   all text and the silhouette
teal         #2799A4   PET COACH label
tiffany      #2BC4C4   one hairline accent per lockup, nothing more
ivory        #FAF9F6   warm background
pearl        #EEF3F3   secondary background

TYPE
Playfair Display 600 — name. never lighter than 500
Montserrat 500 — PET COACH and all functional labels

MINIMUM SIZE
mark 12 mm · badge 22 mm · horizontal lockup 32 mm
below 40 px on screen use the monogram or favicon, not the full silhouette

CLEAR SPACE
keep free space equal to the height of one letter of the wordmark on all sides

NOTE ON SVG TEXT
the wordmark in the SVGs is live text referencing Playfair Display. it renders
correctly in browsers. for a printer or Illustrator, send the PNGs, or ask your
printer to convert text to outlines with Playfair Display installed.
